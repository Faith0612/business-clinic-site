Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026.

At Business Clinic, we understand that sharing details about your business can feel personal.

You might be sharing information about your sales, offers, customers, operations, or things you’ve been struggling to figure out. We don’t take that lightly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and store it, who may have access to it, and the choices you have about your information.

Our goal is to make sure you understand what happens to your information before deciding to share it with us.

Who We Are

Business Clinic is a business diagnosis and consulting service operated by Omoarebun Faith.

If you have questions about this policy or how we handle your information, you can contact us at:

Email: faithosarem@gmail.com

In this policy, “Business Clinic,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the business operating this website and providing its services.

What Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with Business Clinic.

When you apply for a free diagnosis, contact us, or enquire about working together, we may collect:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your business name, website, and social media links.
  • Information about your offers, products, services, and customers.
  • Details about your business challenges, goals, sales patterns, and operations.
  • Information about strategies you’ve tried and the results you’ve experienced.
  • Revenue or other business financial information, where relevant to your application.
  • Information you choose to include in your messages or other communications with us.

We may also collect information needed to arrange a call or process a payment if you decide to purchase a service.

Depending on the website platform and tools we use, certain technical information may also be collected when you visit our website.

This may include information about your device, browser, IP address, and how you interact with the website.

We aim to collect only information relevant to the purpose for which it is needed.

You should not send us passwords, account login credentials, payment card details, or unrelated confidential information as part of your diagnosis application.

Why We Collect Your Information

We use your information to operate Business Clinic and provide the services you’ve requested.

When you apply for a free diagnosis, we use the information you submit to review your application, understand your business situation, and investigate the relevant parts of your business.

We also use your contact information to communicate with you about your application, request clarification where necessary, arrange your diagnosis call, deliver your written findings, and respond to your questions.

If you choose to purchase a service, we may use relevant information to deliver that service, manage our working relationship, process payments through our chosen payment provider, and maintain necessary business records.

We may also use information when necessary to maintain the security of our services, resolve disputes, or meet applicable legal obligations.

We will not use your information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis and any additional notice or consent required by applicable law.

How We Store Your Information

Business Clinic currently uses Google Forms to collect application information and Google Drive and Google Docs to store, review, and prepare business diagnosis documents.

We also communicate with applicants manually through email rather than using an automated email marketing platform at this time.

Depending on how we arrange calls, we may use Google Calendar or Calendly. If you purchase a service, we may use Paystack or another payment provider to process your payment.

These providers operate their own systems and may process or store information as necessary to provide their services.

We will update this policy if we introduce additional tools that materially change how your information is collected, stored, or used.

Who Can Access Your Information?

At present, I personally review applications, investigate the relevant parts of your business, and prepare the written diagnosis summaries.

I do not currently have a team of employees or other people routinely reviewing your applications or diagnosis documents on my behalf.

However, information may also be processed by the third-party providers whose services Business Clinic uses.

For example, Google may process information submitted through Google Forms or stored in Google Drive and Google Docs. Google Calendar or Calendly may process information associated with scheduling a call, depending on which service is used.

If you make a payment, the payment provider may process the information necessary to complete and manage that transaction.

These providers may process information under their own terms, privacy policies, and security arrangements. Their access and processing depend on the services being used and how those services are configured.

We may also disclose information where required by law or where disclosure is otherwise lawfully necessary.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Handling Business-Sensitive Information

We recognise that the information you share about your business may be commercially sensitive.

We aim to use only the information relevant to your application, diagnosis, or any service you choose to purchase.

You do not need to share every private detail of your business to get started. If additional information would help us investigate your situation, we will explain what we need and why it would be useful.

Please avoid including identifiable information about your customers, employees, or other individuals unless it is genuinely necessary. Where possible, use anonymised or aggregated information instead.

We will not use your business information as a public case study, testimonial, or promotional example without obtaining the permission or other lawful basis required for that use.

What Happens If You Don’t Work With Us Afterwards?

Applying for a free diagnosis does not commit you to purchasing a paid service.

You are free to review your findings and decide what you want to do next.

If you choose not to work with us afterwards, we may retain relevant information for a reasonable period where necessary to manage our records, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or protect our legitimate interests, subject to applicable law.

We will not keep information indefinitely simply because you once applied for a diagnosis.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

The appropriate retention period may depend on whether you submitted an application, received a diagnosis, purchased a service, or entered into an ongoing working relationship with us.

We may also need to retain certain records for legal, accounting, or dispute-resolution purposes.

We will periodically review the information we hold and take reasonable steps to delete or securely dispose of information that is no longer needed, subject to applicable legal requirements.

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we handle against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

These steps include limiting access to business documents, using the security features available through our chosen service providers, and avoiding the collection of information that is not needed for the service.

However, no online service or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We cannot promise that information transmitted or stored electronically will never be affected by a security incident.

Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may have rights concerning your personal information.

These may include the right to request access to information we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccurate information, request deletion in certain circumstances, object to certain types of processing, or withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing.

You may also have the right to request a copy of certain information or complain to a relevant data protection authority.

To ask a question about your information or exercise an applicable privacy right, contact us at +234 7039905947

We may need to verify your identity before responding to certain requests.

You can choose not to provide particular information. However, if that information is necessary to investigate your business situation, we may be unable to complete the diagnosis or provide the service you’ve requested.

Cookies and Website Information

Our website platform may collect certain technical information or use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences, or understand how visitors use the website.

The specific information collected will depend on the platform we choose and the tools enabled on the website.

We will update this section to reflect the actual website platform, cookies, analytics tools, and other technologies we use.

Where required by applicable law, we will provide appropriate information and obtain consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

Email Communications

We currently communicate with applicants and clients manually through email.

If you apply for a diagnosis, we may email you about your application, request relevant clarification, deliver your findings, or discuss a service you’ve requested.

Applying for a free diagnosis does not automatically subscribe you to unrelated marketing emails where separate consent is required.

If we introduce a newsletter or automated marketing emails in the future, we will update our practices and provide any required notice, consent options, and unsubscribe facilities.

International Data Transfers

Some of the service providers we use may store or process information in countries other than the country in which you live or submit your information.

If your information is transferred internationally, we will take the steps required by applicable law to ensure that the transfer is lawful and that appropriate protections are in place.

The countries involved and the safeguards used will depend on the providers and services we actually use.

Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to other websites or platforms.

If you visit a third-party website, its own privacy policy and practices will apply to your interaction with it.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of websites we do not operate.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, website, tools, or legal obligations change.

When we make changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent for material changes.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
